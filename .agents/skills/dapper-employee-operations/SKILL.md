# Dapper employee operations
Read canonical LEGACY_INVENTORY.md, requirements matrix and employee-related open decisions. Imported HRMS code and recovered portal frontend are reference material; missing SOP/backend rules do not establish parity.

Keep assignment visibility scoped to the authenticated employee and approved locations. Model generalized vehicle/equipment jobs and retain dealership/fleet identifiers without expanding financial access.

Onboarding, policy receipts, training/assessments, attendance, breaks, timesheet review and overtime require their discovered source rules or explicit approval. Do not claim a polished screen runs these workflows until persisted transitions and negative access cases pass.

GPS collection remains disabled without approved sessions, notice/consent, device expectations, visibility and retention. A browser geolocation/check-in primitive is not proof of continuous background tracking or legal compliance.

Verify phone/tablet workflows and server-side assignment boundaries with synthetic data. Historical employee/attendance/ID-document migration needs separately agreed inventory and access; this skill does not authorize sensitive exports or live employee changes.
