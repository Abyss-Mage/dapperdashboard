## Direction contract
THESIS: The detailer's 50/50 test panel. A strip of masking tape divides each view into before and after, so the site shows the work instead of claiming it. Refuses the category default of a dark photo, a big headline, a red pill and a row of cards.
OWN-WORLD: gloss-black paint ground, inspection-light white highlights, masking-tape tan strips (slightly rotated, knife-cut ends) carrying condensed dark caps as navigation and labels; Dapper red only for booking and the "after" mark; the before side is hazed, desaturated and swirled. Display: Barlow Condensed; body: Manrope.
STORY: The visitor sees the difference, trusts the craft, and books or prices a visit.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Inner pages open on a full-bleed photo split by a vertical tape strip the visitor drags; the title splits across before (grey) and after (white) with the page action under the after side; a grip and a "Drag the tape" cue show it moves. Header is a tape strip across the top with the logo and links written on it and Book in red at its end. Photos that do not show the page's vehicle type are credited as studio photos; vehicle types without photos are tape-labelled panels.
