Frame slicer

Turns the finished hero video into the numbered WebP frames and manifest.json that the home page's scroll story plays. Pick the video, check the settings, then choose the main website/assets/frames folder. Run python build-pages.py afterwards to copy the frames into the web app.

1. Video

No video chosen.

2. Frames

Desktop frames fill the box from the middle of the video. Phone frames are a portrait cut; move the crop centre to keep the car in shot.

3. Where each story step rests (optional)

One step per line, as a time in the video (12.5s), a frame number (120) or end. Leave it empty to spread the nine steps evenly. Step names: clear, glass, tint50, tint35, ppf, satin, nardo, candy, shift (see frames-brief/VEO_PROMPTS.md).
