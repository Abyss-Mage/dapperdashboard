1. Project Overview
Dapper Detailing Pros intends to undertake a complete redevelopment of its existing website and internal employee
management portal to establish a unified, scalable, and franchise-oriented digital platform.
The project will replace the existing WordPress website and BookingPress Pro implementation with a custom-built
website and booking management system. Existing booking data will be retained and migrated to the new platform.
The project will also include a comprehensive revamp of the existing employee portal, incorporating its current
functionality, including employee onboarding, testing, GPS/location tracking, time tracking, dealership tagging, and B2B
job management.
The website, booking system, employee portal, and administrative dashboards will operate as interconnected
components of a centralized platform. The architecture must support multiple franchises and locations under the Dapper
Detailing Pros brand, with controlled access to bookings, employees, customers, financial information, and operational
records.
The platform will integrate Square for payment processing and POS workflows, Apple Calendar synchronization,
customized invoicing, automated email notifications, and Mailchimp for marketing subscription forms and popup
campaigns. The solution must be designed for future expansion as Dapper Detailing Pros grows its franchise network.
2. Project Objectives
1. Completely redesign and rebuild the existing website using a custom user interface and modern web technologies.
 
2. Replace BookingPress Pro with a flexible, custom booking engine.
 
3. Retain and migrate existing booking data from the current system.
 
4. Develop a centralized administrative dashboard for managing the website, bookings, franchises, locations, employees,
 and business operations.
5. Revamp the existing employee portal while preserving its established functionality.
 
6. Introduce role-based and location-based access controls for franchise operations.
 
7. Integrate Square for online payments and supported in-person POS workflows.
 
8. Implement a centralized, customizable invoice generation and management system.
 
9. Synchronize bookings with Apple Calendar across the company, franchise locations, and individual employees.
 
10. Automate customer, employee, and administrator email notifications.
 
11. Integrate Mailchimp with configurable popup forms and newsletter subscription functionality.
 
12. Establish a secure and scalable foundation for franchise expansion and centralized business management.
