## Priority correctness findings from inspected source
1. APPROVED treated as completed settlement: invoices.go lines695/890 and quotes.go1369 accept APPROVED and COMPLETED; invoices webhook1102 also treats APPROVED as settled. Square explicitly documents APPROVED as authorization, with capture changing status to COMPLETED. Consequently an authorization can satisfy recorded paid/deposit amounts before capture. Required fix: pending/authorized state distinct from settled; fulfillment counts COMPLETED only. Add mock APPROVED regression proving no paid/fulfilled result.
2. Webhook persistence errors acknowledged: workspace/square_webhooks.go discards HandleSquareWebhookPayment error and always returns200. Database/audit/transient failure is not persisted but acknowledged. Required fix durable scoped webhook inbox/receipt and retryable failure response; add injected store/audit failure case.
3. Merchant/amount/currency omitted from webhook application: event MerchantID and payment amount/currency are parsed but not passed to HandleSquareWebhookPayment, which finds row by provider_reference alone. Scope, provider merchant/location and expected amount/currency must be bound and verified before balance mutations. Staff RecordSquarePayment also accepts browser SquareLocationID and does not compare response location. Client confirmed separate merchants; do not deploy a global unbound merchant authority.
4. Refund replay arithmetic: invoices.go1170 marks payment refunded after first partial refund; later COMPLETED plus cumulative refunded amount can re-add full original amount before subtracting again, and later REFUNDED-only cumulative increments are ignored once status is refunded. Required immutable provider events/version-aware reconciliation and cumulative refunded delta, including out-of-order/duplicate20→40-of100 test. This is source-derived counterexample, not yet a runtime regression.
5. SMTP transport: internal/email/smtp.go proceeds when STARTTLS is absent and has no socket deadline after DialContext. Invitation secrets may be sent without TLS on a non-authenticated relay; stalled SMTP can exceed context. Fail closed on required TLS, bound all IO/cancellation; test no STARTTLS and stalled greeting. Configuration's InsecureSkipVerify must not become production fallback.

Official provider reference verified during review: https://developer.squareup.com/docs/payments-api/take-payments/card-payments/delayed-capture (APPROVED authorization vs COMPLETED capture). Signature reference: https://developer.squareup.com/docs/webhooks/step3validate. Existing HMAC implementation uses constant-time comparison, raw body and configured URL as required; this does not validate merchant/balance semantics.
